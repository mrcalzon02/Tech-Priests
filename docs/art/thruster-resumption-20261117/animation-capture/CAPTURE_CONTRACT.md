## Verification, resumption and gates

Capture and packaging bind source GLB, prerequisite receipts, profile, canonical camera modules, capture scripts, derivative master and every completed PNG to SHA-256. Resumption refuses mismatched inputs, changed scripts, a changed master, or changed completed images. It resumes only missing matching work. Production refuses a fallback/pilot stage, missing final receiver/aperture evidence, or a failed/unbound GLB validation receipt.

The body and installation well require their complete mandatory handoff before final capture: actual source/export geometry, finite nondegenerate triangles, UVs, PBR/packed images, mating contacts, body clearance/SAT and immutable hash-bound metadata. A preliminary shape or alpha pass is insufficient. The repaired wide well uses the owner's explicit `HANDOFF_COMPLETE`; the failed 19c/19d studies and partial 19c captures remain preserved. Every replacement starts a fresh properly pinned profile/master and genuinely renders its frames.

The capture checks the physical mesh/matrix digest at sampled states, fixed camera, actual source surface arc anchors, per-particle age/position/fade and a genuine rendered wrap. Packaging fully decodes every image, checks clear transparent borders, cold-light zero RGB, exact physical-coverage witness, preserved working-energy coverage, added real arc alpha, meaningful working pixels, shadow black RGB and exact per-cell atlas correspondence. An emission or field failure is retained as a failure; a successful process exit alone is insufficient. A pilot with only selected frames remains partial and does not produce final atlas acceptance.

The next independent gate is native north-only placement and actual layer-clock/state review on disposable platforms. The baseline body stays installed until final source/capture/native evidence warrants promotion. No ordinary save/profile changes, owner visual approval, released art, real background distortion or final family completion is inferred from these source captures.
