# Authored machinery audio V01

Original procedural mono 48kHz SFX: compressor motor/gear roar, valve chatter, loaded housing knocks and pressure chuffs, with start, idle and shutdown cues. The compressor working bed loops over eight seconds. Purifier pump/water and electrolyzer rectifier/process beds have distinct quieter four-second loops. No sampled recordings or third-party music are used.

The audio-only review extension binds all three existing review prototypes without touching accepted geometry or graphics. Compressor gain 1.15, audible distance multiplier 3.5 and priority 16 make it the foreground machinery sound; the other two loops use gains .46/.40 and shorter range. The measured effective RMS is about 8.4 dB above the project generic running sample at its existing .38 gain. Master and encoded decode/headroom/loop checks are in the receipt.

These are authored sound assets and prototype bindings. Owner audition and final ordinary-chain integration remain open. The four remaining precursor machines and three reactors must receive machine-specific audio during their production; do not mark them done with silent prototypes.
