# Open-frame hydroforming fabricator V01 pilot

The owner clarified the process as welded steel plates expanded by high-pressure forming material, then rejected a bulky solid die enclosure. This pilot uses narrow supported edge lands, an open hinged clamping frame and an exposed two-sheet pressure blank. The guided welding torch works along the real plate-edge seam, parks clear, the frame clamps the flanges, and the blank expands while its round pressure neck stays fixed. A grounded tailstock carries the hollow injection mandrel and neck seals. Reservoir/pump hardware, a supported pressure line, plate-loading rollers and the registered cogitator/fluid mouth establish the construction. The first closed-block study remains private and is not promoted.

The editable Blender source and actual imported GLB pass 113 recorded assembly contacts, 12 phase samples of the deformed blank with zero measured world-vertex error, coherent idle/12-second working clips, finite/UV-bearing geometry and loaded maps. The current 31,296 triangles contain no degenerates. Actual exported welding, clamped expansion and exposed formed-vessel views were inspected; the comparison contains those real renders without concept-board pixels.

This is a construction pilot, not final art or pressure certification. Packed donor materials are not dedicated finish. Full moving clearance and pressure-route checks, appropriate welding/pump audio, four-direction final captures and a real native fluid/item prototype are still required. Forming-medium gameplay identity and native port cells remain provisional. Ordinary Factorio inserter access is sufficient; the owner explicitly stopped precision hand-to-socket alignment work on the completed forge.
