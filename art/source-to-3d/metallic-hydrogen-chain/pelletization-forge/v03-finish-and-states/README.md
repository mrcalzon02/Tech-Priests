# Forge V03 finish and state candidate

The real 3x3 press now has a pellet-sized supported auger, offset open hopper throat, retained die flange and continuous visible loading/compression/ejection path. The original pilot feeder was too small for its stock and its discharge struck the bed; both were repaired. A grounded hydraulic reservoir/motor, two supported hoses and drilled coaxial actuator couplings complete the local service circuit. There are no gameplay fluid ports.

Source and actual GLB independently pass quarter-frame stock/all-machine and flight/fixed-machine checks over the eight-second cycle, recorded contacts, guided press clearance, loop closure and 36 local hydraulic core rays each. These are bounded mesh/pose checks, not force, mass-conservation or pressure certification. Actual front/rear/clay, hopper, die loading, compressed press, discharge and hydraulic closeups were inspected.

24 authored maps follow the actual frame edges, hopper descent/corners, press tooling, die annuli, guide/die mounting seats, chute contact lanes and reservoir seams. The editable source, runtime GLB, four idle views, 32 working views, six 64/128/256 atlases and reopened capture master are included. The eight-second four-view MP4 pairs the sampled renders with original forge audio at matching .25-second source phase. The compressor remains louder.

This is a finished-art candidate, not owner acceptance or native/economy completion. Native item processing must preserve the incoming pellet HP, native quality, decay stage and remaining deadline. Ordinary recipe conversion must not refresh it. Actual inserter handling, blocked output, save/reload, sound binding and native playback are next; no additional crucible animation is requested.
