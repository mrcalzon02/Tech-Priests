# V11 reduced sensors and inset plating

Removes the six forward-facing individual barrel sensor pods, their brass bezels, glass lenses and short cables. Retains the main roof optical cluster, two antennas, rear control box and cable trunks.

Adds three inset access plates following the existing inclined front fin, six inset armour panels on the pillar sides, and a rear service panel on the optical head. Each has a raised bevelled plate rim, lower oxide red insert, narrow dark gasket seam and recessed fastener seats with small hex heads. All use the existing committed oxide, iron and steel materials. These are modeled layered recesses within plate rims; the underlying pillar is preserved.

Cannon positions and shape, segmented rubber tracks, hatches, hoppers, retained sensors and source geometry are checked for preservation. Run build_plating.py in Blender 5.0.1, then make_review.py with Pillow. Native Blender, GLB, actual whole views and close-ups are packaged after export reimport.
